Paramount+ Supported Devices – Setup & Activation Guide

Activate Paramount+ Now

Paramount+ works with a wide range of smart TVs and streaming devices, making it easy to watch movies, shows, live sports, and other content on your preferred screen. Before starting, make sure your device is compatible, connected to the internet, and has the latest version of the Paramount+ app.

Which Devices Support Paramount+?

Paramount+ is available on many popular streaming platforms and smart TV systems. Depending on your device and region, you may be able to install the Paramount+ app and sign in directly.

Commonly supported device categories include:

  • Roku devices
  • Amazon Fire TV
  • Apple TV
  • Android TV and Google TV
  • Samsung Smart TVs
  • LG Smart TVs
  • Vizio Smart TVs
  • PlayStation and other supported gaming consoles

Availability can vary by device model, operating system, and location. Check your device's app store to see whether the Paramount+ app is available.

How to Set Up Paramount+ on a Supported Device

Setting up Paramount+ is generally straightforward:

  1. Connect your TV or streaming device to the internet.
  2. Open the device's app store.
  3. Search for the Paramount+ app.
  4. Download and install the app.
  5. Open Paramount+ after installation.
  6. Select Sign In or the available activation option.
  7. Follow the instructions displayed on your TV screen.
  8. If an activation code appears, use the provided activation page to complete the sign-in process.
  9. Return to the Paramount+ app and wait for your account to connect.

Once activation is complete, you can browse the Paramount+ library and start streaming.

Paramount+ Activation by Device

Different devices may use slightly different sign-in or activation steps. For device-specific instructions, follow the guide that matches your setup:

  • Roku: Install Paramount+, open the app, and follow the on-screen activation instructions.
  • Fire TV: Search for Paramount+ in the Amazon Appstore, install it, and sign in.
  • Apple TV: Download Paramount+ from the App Store and complete the sign-in process.
  • Samsung Smart TV: Open the Samsung app store, install Paramount+, and follow the displayed instructions.
  • LG Smart TV: Find Paramount+ in the LG Content Store and complete the setup.
  • Android TV: Download Paramount+ through Google Play and follow the activation instructions.

Paramount+ Device Activation Not Working?

If you cannot activate Paramount+ on your device, try these basic troubleshooting steps:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Restart the Paramount+ app.
  • Restart your TV or streaming device.
  • Make sure the Paramount+ app is updated.
  • Check that your device software is up to date.
  • Generate a new activation code if the previous one is no longer accepted.
  • Make sure you are using the correct Paramount+ account.
  • Reinstall the app if the problem continues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which devices can I use for Paramount+?
Paramount+ supports a variety of smart TVs, streaming devices, mobile platforms, and gaming devices. Availability depends on the specific device and region.
