How Paramount+ Activation Works

Follow these simple steps to connect Paramount+ to your compatible smart TV or streaming device.

1

Open Paramount+

Launch the Paramount+ app on your compatible smart TV, streaming player, console, or other supported device.

2

Choose Sign In

Select the sign-in or activation option displayed on your device screen.

3

Enter the Code

Use the activation code shown on your device and follow the on-screen instructions.

4

Start Streaming

After successful verification, return to your device and begin watching Paramount+ content.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about Paramount+ activation and device setup.

What is ParamountPlus.com/Activate?

It refers to the activation process used to connect the Paramount+ service with a compatible streaming device.

Where can I find my activation code?

Open the Paramount+ application on your supported device. The app may display an activation code during the sign-in process.

What if my activation code does not work?

Check the code carefully and make sure it is still valid. You can also restart the app and request a new code.

Can I activate Paramount+ on different devices?

Paramount+ is available across many supported platforms. The exact activation process may vary slightly by device.

Why is the Paramount+ app not loading?

Check your internet connection, restart the application, and make sure your device software and app are up to date.
